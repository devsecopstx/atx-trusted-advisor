# gtm-client-acquisition checklist

## Before any outbound

- [ ] ICP is IA desk or HNWI operator (not retail signal-seeker)
- [ ] First two lines are personalized
- [ ] Offer is trial + 20-min walkthrough (not “demo our AI”)
- [ ] No invented metrics, testimonials, or live-broker claims
- [ ] Copy says Investment Advisor / IA (not RIA)
- [ ] Disclaimer if a strategy example is included

## Daily loop

- [ ] Read `gtm-pipeline.md` before writing
- [ ] 2 X drafts, 3 lead notes, 1 outreach, 1 founder action
- [ ] Append dated brief — do not wipe prior days
- [ ] Did **not** publish or send

## Weekly (founder)

- [ ] 10 personalized notes sent
- [ ] 3 proof posts published by a human
- [ ] Pipeline updated with replies / calls
- [ ] One intro call completed or scheduled
