## Usage Rules

1. Keep dark surfaces dominant; accents should remain secondary.
2. Use cyan and violet only for intent and hierarchy, not decoration.
3. Maintain WCAG-friendly contrast for all text over dark surfaces.
4. Avoid full-canvas neon gradients and high-saturation backgrounds.
5. Prefer neutral ramps for charts; reserve accent colors for key signals.
