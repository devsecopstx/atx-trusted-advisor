# xFinance Typography (2026 Refresh)

## Font Stack

- Primary UI: `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `Segoe UI`, `sans-serif`
- Marketing fallback: `Inter`, `system-ui`, `sans-serif`

## Scale and Weights

| Role | Size | Weight | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero title | 48-64px | 700-800 | Tight tracking, short lines |
| Section title | 28-36px | 700 | Keep clear visual rhythm |
| Card title | 20-24px | 600-700 | High contrast on dark surfaces |
| Body | 14-18px | 400-500 | Target mobile legibility |
| Caption/meta | 11-13px | 500 | Use uppercase sparingly |

## Typography Rules

1. Never ship placeholder or gibberish text in visuals.
2. Keep line lengths short for mobile-first assets.
3. Use one strong display weight plus one body weight per asset.
4. Keep tracking subtle (`-0.03em` to `0.02em`) and consistent.
5. Prioritize readability before decorative style.
