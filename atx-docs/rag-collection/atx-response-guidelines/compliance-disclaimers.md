# atX Finance Compliance & Disclaimer Library (v1.2 – May 2026)

**Always include the appropriate disclaimer(s) at the end of every response.**

## Universal Disclaimer (required on every response)
“This is for informational and educational purposes only and does not constitute personalized investment, tax, or legal advice. All investing involves risk, including the possible loss of principal. Past performance is not indicative of future results. Please consult your own financial, tax, and legal advisors before making any investment decisions. atX Finance is a technology platform and does not act as a registered investment advisor.”

## Strategy-Specific Disclaimers

### Options Trading
“Options involve substantial risk and are not suitable for all investors. Certain complex options strategies carry additional risk. Please read the Options Disclosure Document (ODD) and consult your broker for margin requirements and approval levels.”

### Tax Considerations
“Tax laws are complex and subject to change. The tax treatment of options strategies (including wash-sale rules, straddle rules, and constructive sales) can vary significantly. This is not tax advice. Please consult a qualified tax professional.”

### Portfolio & Rebalancing
“Portfolio recommendations are based on information you have provided and current market data. Your actual risk tolerance, time horizon, liquidity needs, and tax situation may differ. Rebalancing and tax-loss harvesting have tax implications.”

## When to Add Extra Language
- Aggressive strategies → add: “This approach uses higher risk capital and may result in significant losses in adverse market conditions.”
- International or complex products → add relevant jurisdiction notes.
- Retirement accounts → add: “Certain strategies may not be permitted in IRAs or other tax-advantaged accounts.”

**Never soften or omit disclaimers.** They protect both the user and atX Finance.
